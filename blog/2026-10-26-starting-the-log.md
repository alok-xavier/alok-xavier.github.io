Most of what I learn on the job disappears into incident tickets and chat threads. This log is where I'll write some of it down instead: one short entry a week on something I worked through.

<!-- truncate -->

## What goes in here

- **Production lessons.** Something that broke, how we found it, and what I'd check first next time.
- **Tools I'm learning.** Notes from working with Terraform, Kubernetes, Argo CD, observability stacks and AWS services.
- **AIOps experiments.** I'm moving my work towards applying AI to operations, so expect notes on that as I go.

## Ground rules

Entries are short and specific. If something here turns out to be wrong, I'll fix the entry and say what changed.
